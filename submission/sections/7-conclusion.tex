\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We proved that exact edge cover by non-strict temporal walks is polynomially solvable for prescribed start counts, through M-convexity after removing an even component's dead state, and that the strict variants are NP-complete even with prescribed end vertices. The bound $O(D(n+sm))$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:time} is an upper bound on growth. On the shortage family the measured growth is slightly above $m^2$, and certificates from the augmentation loop rest on Lemma~\ref{lem:gamma}(b) and Lemma~\ref{lem:localmax}. We made no comparison with Boolean satisfiability or a generic submodular flow solver. Future work includes minimizing the number of walks with free starts, strict walks with at most two edges per vertex in each snapshot, and the dependence of $D$ on the initial flow.
